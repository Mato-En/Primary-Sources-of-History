The Epic of Gilgamesh is a story about the king of Uruk, who befriends Enriku and together they face several tribulations. After Enriku dies, he is frightened of death and seeks eternal life. This quest is not successful and he returns home empty handed.

This epic provides a glimpse into cultural values and practices at that time, like the relationship to the gods or the roles of women.
